Tata Nexon: The Safety-First Compact Champion

The Tata Nexon continues to dominate the compact SUV market by offering unparalleled safety and futuristic aesthetics. With its latest facelift, it brings features typically found in premium luxury cars to the masses. Setting a benchmark in build quality, it is the go-to choice for families seeking security alongside high technology.

Exterior Design: Futuristic Split-Face Style

The Nexon has transitioned to a cutting-edge design profile. The front is defined by high-set bi-functional LED DRLs, a sleek black front grille trim, and bumper-mounted projector headlights. The coupe-like sloping roofline, floating contrasting roof colours, and 16-inch aerodynamic alloy wheels combine to offer a highly modern crossover appeal.

The rear design stands out with its distinct X-shaped LED tail lights connected by a light bar, featuring sequential swipe indicators. Combined with a robust ground clearance of 208mm, it makes traversing high water crossings and rocky trails entirely hassle-free.

Interior Cabin and Next-Gen Cockpit

Inside the cabin, the Nexon represents a massive digital overhaul. It is equipped with a two-spoke steering wheel featuring an illuminated Tata logo, a first-in-segment feature. Dual 10.25-inch high-resolution screens serve as the digital instrument panel and floating touchscreen infotainment center, supporting wireless connectivity and navigation mirroring.

Ventilated leatherette front seats, a voice-command enabled electric sunroof, a wireless charging pad, and an air purifier with a AQI display ensure high passenger convenience. The touch-based climate control panel with toggle switches adds to the sleek cockpit vibe.

Engine Choice, Gearboxes & DCA Smoothness

Buyers can choose between two turbo-charged engines: a 1.2-liter Revotron turbo petrol producing 120 PS, and a 1.5-liter Revotorq diesel delivering 115 PS. Gearbox selections are the most comprehensive in the segment: a 5-speed manual, a 6-speed manual, a 6-speed AMT, and a premium 7-speed DCA (Dual Clutch Automatic) with paddle shifters.

The DCA transmission shifts gears in milliseconds, resolving the sluggish performance typical of traditional AMTs. The drive modes (Eco, City, Sport) physically alter throttle response and steering weight, adapting to different driving environments.

Uncompromising Five-Star Safety

Safety has long been Tata Nexon's key hallmark, having earned a top 5-star adult occupant safety rating from Global NCAP. Structural enhancements are complemented by active features, including standard 6 airbags, electronic stability program (ESP), ISOFIX anchors, and hill hold control. Higher models also feature a 360-degree surround camera system and a blind-view monitor to navigate narrow city lanes safely.

Final Verdict

The Tata Nexon is the most well-rounded compact SUV for those who won't compromise on safety or technology. It delivers an excellent balance of engine refinement, safety shields, and high-tech features that make every journey a premium experience.

Tata Nexon Questions & Answers

Q: What is the mileage of the Tata Nexon Diesel?

The Nexon Diesel MT offers a best-in-class mileage of approximately 24.1 kmpl as per ARAI standards.

Q: Does the Nexon feature ADAS technology?

While not a full Level 2 ADAS, it features several safety monitors and advanced ESP with electronic traction control.

Q: Is the petrol DCA (Dual Clutch) better than the AMT?

Yes, the 7-speed DCA offers much smoother and faster gear shifts compared to the budget-oriented 6-speed AMT.

Q: How many airbags are offered in the Nexon?

Tata now offers 6 airbags as standard across all variants of the Nexon, ensuring top-tier safety for all passengers.

Q: Does it have ventilated seats?

Yes, the top-spec Fearless and Creative trims come with front ventilated seats for added comfort in Indian summers.

Q: Can I link it to my smartwatch?

Yes, Tata's IRA 2.0 connected car suite supports smartwatch integration for remote locks and analytics.
